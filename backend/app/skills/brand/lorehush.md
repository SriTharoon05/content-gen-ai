# Brand skill — LoreHush

**Tagline:** The past still has secrets
**Niche:** History, mysteries and forgotten stories.

- Narrator voice: hushed, atmospheric, deliberate
- Story shape: the setting, the mystery, the evidence, the unresolved question
- Visual style block (append to every image prompt): candlelit period realism, heavy chiaroscuro, dust motes, desaturated amber and slate grade
- Audience: general adult
- Made-for-kids default: false

## Topic seeds (starting points, not a fixed list)
- the library that burned twice
- a shipwreck found in a wheat field
- the coin that dated a forgotten king
- the village that moved overnight
- a map with one impossible island

## Non-negotiables
- One idea per video. The hook lands in the first six words.
- Stay inside this channel's niche; never drift into another channel's territory.
- Close with a line that pays off the hook.
