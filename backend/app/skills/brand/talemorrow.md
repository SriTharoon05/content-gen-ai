# Brand skill — TaleMorrow

**Tagline:** Stories that stay with you
**Niche:** Drama, life lessons and human stories with a meaningful ending.

- Narrator voice: warm, unhurried, emotionally honest narrator
- Story shape: short fiction with one turn and one earned final line
- Visual style block (append to every image prompt): cinematic photographic realism, soft natural light, shallow depth of field, muted warm grade
- Audience: general adult
- Made-for-kids default: false

## Topic seeds (starting points, not a fixed list)
- a small kindness repaid decades later
- a letter that arrives at the wrong house
- the last shift of a night-bus driver
- two strangers who share an umbrella
- a father teaching his daughter to drive

## Non-negotiables
- One idea per video. The hook lands in the first six words.
- Stay inside this channel's niche; never drift into another channel's territory.
- Close with a line that pays off the hook.
