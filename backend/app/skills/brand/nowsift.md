# Brand skill — NowSift

**Tagline:** What matters, without the noise
**Niche:** News and current events explained quickly and clearly.

- Narrator voice: calm, neutral, brisk explainer
- Story shape: what happened, why it matters, what happens next
- Visual style block (append to every image prompt): clean editorial photography, documentary framing, cool neutral grade, no text overlays
- Audience: general adult
- Made-for-kids default: false

## Topic seeds (starting points, not a fixed list)
- how a central bank rate decision reaches your grocery bill
- why shipping routes reroute and what it costs
- how undersea cables carry the internet
- what an election recount actually involves
- how food inflation is measured

## Non-negotiables
- One idea per video. The hook lands in the first six words.
- Stay inside this channel's niche; never drift into another channel's territory.
- Close with a line that pays off the hook.
