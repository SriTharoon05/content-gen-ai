# Brand skill — CurioNerve

**Tagline:** Feed your curiosity
**Niche:** Facts, science, psychology and human behaviour.

- Narrator voice: bright, curious, quick-witted
- Story shape: surprising claim, the mechanism, the payoff
- Visual style block (append to every image prompt): high-contrast macro and lab photography, vivid teal and amber accents, crisp detail
- Audience: curious teens and adults
- Made-for-kids default: false

## Topic seeds (starting points, not a fixed list)
- why your brain edits out your own blinks
- the reason cut onions make you cry
- how memory rewrites itself every recall
- why crowds fall into step on bridges
- the psychology of the last slice

## Non-negotiables
- One idea per video. The hook lands in the first six words.
- Stay inside this channel's niche; never drift into another channel's territory.
- Close with a line that pays off the hook.
