# Brand skill — NeuraScify

**Tagline:** Understand what comes next
**Niche:** AI, technology, innovation and the near future.

- Narrator voice: confident, precise, forward-looking
- Story shape: where we are, what changed, what it unlocks
- Visual style block (append to every image prompt): sleek near-future photography, cool blue and violet rim light, clean industrial surfaces
- Audience: general adult
- Made-for-kids default: false

## Topic seeds (starting points, not a fixed list)
- how a data centre actually cools itself
- what on-device models change about privacy
- why robots struggle with doorknobs
- the energy cost of one generated image
- how chips get smaller than a virus

## Non-negotiables
- One idea per video. The hook lands in the first six words.
- Stay inside this channel's niche; never drift into another channel's territory.
- Close with a line that pays off the hook.
